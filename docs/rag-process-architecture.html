RAG Workbench process architecture

Retrieval-first LangGraph workflow · data, models and governance

01 / Data

  • SEC EDGAR and XBRL filings are extracted, structure-chunked and embedded with Qwen3-Embedding into DuckDB.
  • Polygon metadata lands in polygon_tickers and is read directly, not chunked or embedded.

02 / Retrieval and branching

  • Retrieval is the LangGraph entry node: BM25 + vector search, RRF fusion, candidate top-k 3, cross-encoder rerank to 5, relevance filter.
  • The classifier then routes numeric questions through XBRL eval, Polars math and verification to output or abstention, and qualitative questions to an LLM answer with tool calling.

03 / Governance

  • Output, abstention and qualitative answers all pass through build_lineage: audit trail for every run, review queue on sampled or escalated routes, then human review and calibration.
  • Graph RAG is a separate /api/chat/graph-rag workflow. DeepSeek and MiMo answer; Qwen3-Embedding and the cross-encoder support retrieval.
